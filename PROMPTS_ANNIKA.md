# Annika Reuter — fertige Prompts

Papierrestauratorin, 38. Die zweite Person. Alles hier ist zum Einsetzen
gedacht: jeder Prompt steht für sich, du musst nichts zusammensetzen.

**Am besten gibst du ein fertiges Tobias-Bild als Stilreferenz mit** —
dann trifft der Strich sicher. Ohne Referenz genügen die Prompts auch,
sie beschreiben den Stil vollständig.

Dateien gehören nach `bilder/` und heißen genau so, wie sie unten stehen.
Fehlt eine, zeigt das Spiel das Grundporträt und die Stimmung als Wort —
kaputt geht nichts.

## Der schnelle Weg: ein Kontaktbogen für alle zehn

Ein Bild, zehn Felder, danach zerschneiden. So ist auch Tobias entstanden.

> A contact sheet of ten portrait panels in a 5 by 2 grid, all of the same
> woman, illustrated in a flat vector-comic style: clean black line art,
> soft cel shading, muted natural palette, warm skin tones, light grey
> background (#e2e2e0) in every panel, head-and-shoulders, front view, thin
> even gutters between panels, no text, no labels, no borders around the
> whole sheet.
>
> The woman, identical in all ten panels: 38 years old, very fair skin,
> faint pink around the nose and eyelids, pale ash-blond hair, fine and
> grown out, gathered in a low practical knot with two strands escaped at
> the temples. Very light eyebrows and lashes, no make-up. A magnifying
> loupe headband pushed up onto her hair, lenses flipped up. A light grey
> cotton conservator's coat, sleeves pushed to the elbow, over a soft
> high-necked oatmeal jumper. Thin blue nitrile gloves, the right one half
> peeled off. A pencil behind her right ear. Small plain silver studs and a
> thin chain with a ring hanging on it. Slight, narrow-shouldered.
>
> The ten panels differ only in expression, in this order, left to right,
> top row then bottom row:
> 1 — attentive and very still, mouth closed, eyes directed slightly past
> the viewer rather than at them.
> 2 — a small closed-lip smile that arrives a beat late, eyes warm, one
> shoulder relaxing.
> 3 — laughing, covering her mouth with the back of her gloved hand, eyes
> creased shut, shoulders pulled up towards her ears.
> 4 — leaning in, the loupe flipped down over her right eye, both eyebrows
> raised, fully absorbed, lips slightly parted.
> 5 — looking down at her own hands, turning the ring on its chain between
> two fingers, mouth closed.
> 6 — brows drawn very slightly together, head angled away while her eyes
> stay on the viewer, lips pressed thin, one hand at her collar.
> 7 — rigidly polite, jaw tight, high colour on both cheeks, eyes cast down
> at the table, chin level; annoyance kept in, not shown.
> 8 — shoulders drawn in, arms held close to her body, chin lowered so the
> loose strands fall forward like a curtain, gaze turned to the side.
> 9 — eyes wet and bright, a quick involuntary smile, already looking down
> and tucking a loose strand behind her ear to cover it.
> 10 — standing, half turned away in three-quarter view, both palms flat on
> a table, eyes on the sheet of paper in front of her and not on the viewer.

## Ihr Raum

`bilder/raum_annika.webp` — Querformat 16:9, liegt hinter dem Seitenkopf.

> Illustrated interior in the same flat vector-comic style as the portraits:
> clean black line art, soft cel shading, muted natural palette, no people,
> no text, 16:9.
>
> A paper conservation workshop in the basement of a state archive. No
> daylight; two long adjustable lamps over a large white flat table. On the
> table a single sheet of foxed paper held down by six small brass weights,
> tweezers and a bone folder laid out exactly parallel, a jar of wheat
> starch paste, a stack of translucent Japanese tissue. Behind it a suction
> table and a flat file cabinet with every drawer labelled in the same neat
> hand. A pegboard with tools hung on painted outlines, one outline empty.
> On a shelf a small framed group photograph and a pot plant that is not
> doing well. A printed schedule on the wall with every line ticked off
> except the last. Cool, quiet, extremely orderly.

Wer lieber die Szene mit ihr darin will, wie `raum.webp` bei Tobias, hängt
an denselben Prompt an:

> The woman from the portraits sits at the flat table in three-quarter view
> from behind, the loupe flipped down, working on the sheet.

## Falls du den Stil nachziehen musst

Die Tobias-Bilder haben: kräftige, gleichmäßige schwarze Konturen, weiche
Zellschattierung ohne Verläufe, gedämpfte Naturfarben mit einem warmen
Akzent (bei ihm Ziegelrot), hellgrauer, völlig leerer Hintergrund,
Brustbild mittig, quadratisch. Ihr warmer Akzent ist das Haferbeige des
Pullovers und das Blau der Handschuhe — bewusst kühler als bei ihm, damit
man die beiden im Menü sofort auseinanderhält.
